# War Domain creation proficiencies

War creation offered martial equipment but finalization ignored its proficiency
grants. Apply heavy armor and the runtime martial proficiency to new level-one
War Clerics. Preserve base Cleric grants and Life behavior. Other domains and
War Priest are out of scope. Existing saved characters are not silently rewritten.

The runtime martial grant covers both melee and ranged weapons; picker category
strings cannot be used as runtime proficiency identifiers.
