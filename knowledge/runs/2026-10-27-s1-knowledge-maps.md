## Decided

- `examples/*` modules are drawn at the root in their own "spikes, never tagged" group, because `examples/` has no map and every module appears in exactly one; the tagger already skips them ([`auto-tag-modules.sh`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/c355102dffe40fa6aa3852d13708ec21c055172d/scripts/auto-tag-modules.sh#L155)) — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- `npc` sits in a "Content" layer of its own rather than in World, because it imports no runtime system and `world` does not import it ([`npc/doc.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/c355102dffe40fa6aa3852d13708ec21c055172d/npc/doc.go)) — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- `game`, `items`, `rpgerr` and `dice` stay in Core, as the overview's module table places them — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- The check also fails a module drawn in a map other than the nearest one above it, because "composes, never redraws" is otherwise unenforced when a module moves from a level map to the root — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- A module node is a rectangle whose label begins with its path; level boxes are `[[…]]`, outside things are `(…)`, subgraph titles are skipped. The check reads nodes, not arrows; arrows are direct `go.mod` requires and held by review — [rpg-project#691](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/691).
- Arrows into `core` are omitted at the root for legibility; every other direct require among root-drawn modules is drawn — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- The dnd5e map draws one dashed arrow against the import direction, encounter asking session for capabilities, and splits every seam into its two directions; the play map states one pair of lines for all six leaf pairs, since no leaf imports another — [rule 15](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
- `character/` is named in the dnd5e map's rules node and prose, not drawn as a node, because it is a package of the `rulebooks/dnd5e` module — [rpg-project#691](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/691).
- `runs/` is created now, by this record; `findings/` is not created, because no finding entry needs it yet — [rule 13](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/main/ideas/team-memory/design.md#standing-rules).
