## Decided

- The json/v2 tag that flattens the bag into the blob is `embed`; `inline` is ignored without error and nests the bag under `Bag`. The design's text said `inline` and was corrected the same day — [`machine/blob.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/blob.go), design "What this costs".
- `anchor_round` sets `round_activated` only while it is zero and the beat carries a round, and always sets `saw_turn_end`; it never re-anchors. The same act on `raging`'s upkeep transition is today's late anchoring — [`machine/acts.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/acts.go), [`e0251231`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/commit/e0251231), design "What a condition looks like".
- `derive_turns_active` runs on the graced turn too, so `turns_active` reads 1 there as today's `raging.go` does — [`e715a8a1`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/commit/e715a8a1).
- The transition event is `Transitioned`, since `Transition` is the recipe struct; the machine exposes an `OnChanged` hook for trackers and the composer carries the `End` hook the plug supplies, because the machine knows no removal topic — [`machine/machine.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/machine.go), [`vocabulary.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/vocabulary.go).
- `Validate` and `Compile` take the bag keys that only output guards read, so the composer can declare them and the machine still refuses a key nothing reads; `Validate` refuses a bag key that collides with a blob header key — [`machine/validate.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/validate.go).
- The class-level leaf reads its class as an int fact of the vocabulary; no new field — [`machine/guard.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/guard.go).
- The first vocabulary is exactly what Rage reads: ops `eq` and `gte`, and `gte` on a string fact is refused at Validate — ruling 4, [`machine/validate_test.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/machine/validate_test.go).

## Found

- Guard answer 119 ns/op; a tracker plus a turn-end beat 618 ns/op, a tracker alone 214 ns/op, so a turn end is about 400 ns with three acts. At table scale this is nothing — [`bench_test.go`](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/e715a8a1922f118515cf4eb69c6b778ec0873488/examples/effects/bench_test.go).
- The design's Rage recipe drifted from `raging.go` in two places before this slice caught it: no `turns_active` on the graced turn, and no late anchoring. Both fixed in the design and here — [raging.go at base](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/conditions/raging.go#L294-L368).
- A byte-equal blob between the old type and the recipe is impossible as the plan first wrote it: the new blob carries a `state` key. S2's test compares after removing `state` — plan task 8.
- Today's `RagingData.Source` is a string ref; the blob's `Source` is a `*core.Ref`. S2's loader accepts both — [raging.go at base](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/conditions/raging.go#L30-L39).

The CI lint job on this PR fails until rpg-toolkit#2145 step 0 lands — [`.golangci-version` at base](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/.golangci-version).
