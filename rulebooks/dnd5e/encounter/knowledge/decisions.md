- 2026-10-27 · the audience of a beat is who witnesses it: members witness things, an ally can be seen getting shot, an NPC can protect. - 2026-10-29 · a row reads only its creature's own holdings: sight testimony (which can be wrong), deeds against it, its side or by it, sounds heard, facts known, and its budget (CanAttack, CanMove, CanDash); it cannot read hit points, down, region or room, stance, or the clock.
