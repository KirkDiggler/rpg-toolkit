## Decisions

- 2026-08-10 · the composable encounter is the foundation the rest is built on, and it is protected: the old way, an encounter that holds behaviour and computes answers itself, is not reached for again · options: the behaviour-holding encounter, the composable encounter · because: the composable format is the foundation the rest builds on · [rpg-project#676](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/676) · upheld: the operator and the lead session have each reached for the old way since 2026-08 and were stopped (operator testimony, 2026-10-27); those PRs are not linked yet
- 2026-10-27 · C8 ("no randomness in the composition") is amended in daylight rather than restored: no random at all is too hard a line, and the direction is a roller seeded per encounter, so rolls are deterministic but random (controlled random); the amendment names its date, this ruling and the drift it legitimises, and AGENTS.md and the design say the roller exists · options: restore C8 and remove the roller, amend C8 to a seeded roller · because: the encounter has held an optional dice.Roller since [rpg-project#465](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/465), so the line was already crossed · [rpg-project#694](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/694) · upheld: none yet
- 2026-10-28 · the measurement of how much of this module is dnd5e-specific is recorded as the baseline and no generic core is built ahead of a use case: go.mod names no rulebook, about 68% of non-test lines touch no D&D concept, about 10% interprets D&D in seven places (the 5e turn budget in the clock file, hearing as passive Perception against a DC, Stealth against passive Perception, the sheet facts, two skill names mapped to deeds, 5e's three-valued passage rule, a short rest is an hour) woven into generic files at six points (the sight answer, the fight loop, the persisted blob, the capability list, verb side effects, the record vocabulary); the path to generic is the capability pattern law C1 already uses, each remaining D&D spot becoming a supplied answer as sneaking's CheckResolver is · options: opening an idea folder for a generic encounter now · because: a generic core is built when a use case needs it, and this table is what that use case will have to split (the operator, 2026-10-28) · [rpg-project#694, two questions](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/694#issuecomment-6462060901) · upheld: none yet
- 2026-10-28 · hold and take are different verbs by design: hold is temporary, the thing never enters inventory, it occupies one or both hands and while held blocks a weapon or a shield, so it is a visible state of the body whose audience is who can see the holder (under party sharing, the union: if any party member sees it, everyone knows); take (loot) puts the thing in inventory, and its everyone audience is the pre-playtest stance that narrows under the encounter's audience policy, never one verb at a time · options: aligning loot and the roll window to the sight sites now · because: hold's sight audience (mutableWitnesses) is intentional; loot's everyone is the pre-playtest stance (the operator, 2026-10-28) · [rpg-project#694, two questions](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/694#issuecomment-6462060901) · upheld: none yet

## Findings

- 2026-10-28 · encounter AGENTS.md still says of randomness "There is no roller here and no place to add one" ([AGENTS.md L41](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/c355102dffe40fa6aa3852d13708ec21c055172d/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/AGENTS.md#L41)), while Capabilities holds `Roller dice.Roller` ([capabilities.go:83](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/c355102dffe40fa6aa3852d13708ec21c055172d/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/capabilities.go#L83)); the C8 amendment above is not yet written there
- 2026-10-27 · the audience of a beat is who witnesses it: members witness things, an ally can be seen getting shot, an NPC can protect. That stance is the game's own and tied to no D&D rule; pre-playtest it is "everyone sees everything" · [rpg-project#658](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/658)
- 2026-10-29 · a row reads only its creature's own holdings: sight testimony (which can be wrong), deeds against it, its side or by it, sounds heard, facts known, and its budget (CanAttack, CanMove, CanDash); it cannot read hit points, down, region or room, stance, or the clock. A member going down lands no deed and mints no fact, so no row can react to a named death today; `down` exists only on arrives, until and endings. [rpg-project#716](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/716) proposes a `fell` deed landed on witnesses so the shenanigans law (testimony, never a flag) holds · [encounter/facts.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/facts.go#L244), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
- 2026-10-29 · `allied` is a static, inert stance: an `until` on an allied pair is refused with "an allied pair has nothing to become", behaviour never reads allied, each pair gets exactly one hostile-to-neutral or neutral-to-hostile flip, and no selector names an ally (selectors are enemy, attacker, actor, sound, a cell, a route). A faction neutral to the party and hostile to its foe already fights that foe in the party's fight (the castle's three-sided fight), so "help the party" exists by opposition and "follow the party" does not; [rpg-project#717](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/717) (companions) and [rpg-project#718](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/718) (survivors talk; a second flip) hang on this · [encounter/disposition.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/disposition.go#L150), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
- 2026-10-29 · no creature opens a door: a driven walk routes only through open doors, so every patrol (`toward: { path }`), march (`toward: { at }`) and pursuit stops at the first shut door, and the castle authors the throne door open for the AI's sake. The primitive question is [rpg-toolkit#2069](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/issues/2069); [rpg-project#715](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/715) is the slice that answers it (closed-unlocked doors open on the step; locked doors still stop; keys are not that slice) · [encounter/directive.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/directive.go#L100), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
- 2026-10-29 · PredicateSpec has exactly five forms, round, down, fact, stance and holds, and no `not` (its doc comment still says four); `requires: { fact }` on an option reads the SPEAKER's knowledge, not the spoken-to creature's, and `holds` is legal only on an option's requires. Room-local social consequences (a failed theft closing one room's lines) are authorable only by a per-room fact plus a `not` form that does not exist yet; [rpg-project#719](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/719) adds it and lane D3 asks whose knowledge requires should read · [dungeonspec/predicate.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/dungeonspec/predicate.go#L50), [dungeonspec/spec.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/dungeonspec/spec.go#L1092-L1094), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
- 2026-10-29 · "cast" is not a key in the single-room dialect: a creature is three pieces joined by id, a template (a stat block over a base rulebook monster: abilities, hitDice, armor, proficiency, skills, actions in order; never HP, AC or attack bonus), a monsterDeclaration (identity: cell, facing, appearanceRef) and a monsterBinding (orders: faction, table, on, temper, actions/addActions/dropActions, options, holds, arrives). Weapon order matters: the driver uses the first action whose target is in reach · [dungeonspec/single_room.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/dungeonspec/single_room.go#L216), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
- 2026-10-29 · row order is not priority in live play: the die covers the sum of eligible weights times temper percent, so two eligible weight-1 rows split 50/50 (for example gate-patrol's alarm row against its guard-base group). The castle tests use a die that always lands 1, which makes the first eligible row win there and can be misread as a rule · [encounter/answer.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/answer.go#L46), [castletest/castle_king_bundles_test.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/dd2ccc8b9a0fc0666194c544a2a6c89671dca12f/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/castletest/castle_king_bundles_test.go#L145), survey [rpg-project#714](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/714)
