## Decided

- A creature never opens a locked door in this slice. This applies lane ruling D5 — [rpg-project#722](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/722).
- The open costs the creature nothing beyond the step. It spends no world action and no turn budget, which is the slice's ruling — [rpg-project#715](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/715).
- A walk nobody chose opens nothing: a push, a pull, a rout from `Direct`, and the public `Route`. Being shoved into a door is not opening it. Route's existing callers, such as the castle gate test's hero routes, keep their answers — [`directive.go`](../../encounter/directive.go).
- The threat zone, the estimate of how far a foe can reach, still treats a shut door as closed — [`dash.go`](../../encounter/dash.go).
- The walk opens the door BEFORE it announces the step through. A step paused for a reaction then resumes through an open door — [`walkdoor.go`](../../encounter/walkdoor.go).
- The driven open does not refresh sight itself. On the turn clock the walk's one settle refreshes it, and now counts an opened door as well as a step. On the world clock the verb that raised the clock refreshes it, so no fight forms halfway through a world round, as `settleWalkOn` already rules — [`clocks.go`](../../encounter/clocks.go).
- The door beat carries `cause` only when the walk has one: `toward`, `away` or `search` on a Routed walk, and `dash`. A plain `Move` names no cause, by the Move arm's own law, so a patrol leg's or a pursuit's open names the actor alone — [`walkdoor.go`](../../encounter/walkdoor.go).
- A door is opened only when it alone closes the step. That excludes a second door, a table across the same crossing, a hidden door the walker has not found, and anything else on the cell — [`walkdoor.go`](../../encounter/walkdoor.go).
