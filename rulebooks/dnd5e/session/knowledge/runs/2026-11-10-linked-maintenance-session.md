# Linked maintenance through session persistence

## Shipped

- Supply a stable current-act/application ordinal to native casts, carry maintenance facts into selectors/information/turn reads, and route death-save actor/dirty peers through the one landing after loading affected holders — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).
- Exercise Manager.Cast and React over JSON-round-tripped stores and a fresh manager: an earlier Bane recipient stays delivered while another target answers, the same application resumes, each verdict is told once, and payment is not repeated — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).

## Decided

- Session supplies identity and custody, not concentration or dice rules. Root maintenance queries supply presence/errors and descriptions; current typed part declarations remain mechanical selector data, while effect rows remain outside selector identity — [R11](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/pull/2323#issuecomment-6755782119).
- Adopt resolution catch-up 6618456d rather than downgrade main's Sites seam; the host remains on its current site/checkpoint graph with root 76aeebcb and effects de822f5e — [native adoption](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/pull/2344).
- Use explicit complete current owner/recipient fixtures where mutation is exercised, and semantic ref/source matching for opaque contribution IDs. Read-only recipient information may describe its local effect without fabricating an owner countdown — [approved design](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/pull/2323).

## Found

- Removing the caller application identity or dropping dirty character persistence makes the real session window/reload regression fail; exact restoration passes. The test distinguishes provider behavior, host persistence and fresh-manager continuation from browser acceptance — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).
- A prior world-clock rows fixture had its Bless cleared by Launch's long rest, so it passed without the condition it claimed to exercise. Restoring the real precondition exposes the already-supported world-priced attack row; unrelated declarations still carry no attack rows — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).
- The landing-only structural test rejected a second dirty-peer save path in DeathSave. The verb now uses the existing landing to save, record and commit; no structural exemption was added — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).

## Mistaken

- The first death-save adoption called saveDirty outside the one landing; the structural gate caught the misplaced orchestration before publication — [S14](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/904).
